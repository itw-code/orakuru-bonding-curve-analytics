---

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

In bonding curve launchpads, raw block explorer views are insufficient. The team needs to answer three questions within 5 seconds:
1. **Capital Velocity**: Which curves are filling fastest, and is the volume organic or bot-churned?
2. **Adversarial Risk**: Are snipers or the token creator dominating the supply before graduation, exposing retail to post-migration dumps?
3. **Graduation Health**: What % of launched tokens graduate to DEX liquidity pools, and what is the protocol fee yield?

This specification maps the dimensional models (`fct_bonding_trades`, `fct_curve_daily_performance`, `dim_wallets`, `dim_tokens`) to actionable operational widgets.

## 3. Panel Breakdown & Metric Definitions

### Panel 1: Protocol Command Center (Top KPIs)
- **Active Curves**: Count of tokens currently on the bonding curve with `curve_status = 'ACTIVE'`.
- **Graduation Rate**: `(Graduated Curves / Total Created Curves) * 100`. Benchmark: $>10\%$ indicates strong launch quality.
- **24h Protocol Fee Yield**: Sum of `protocol_fee_amount` across all buy/sell transactions. Direct protocol revenue.
- **Organic vs Bot Volume Share**: Ratio of volume generated by `RETAIL_ORGANIC` + `ACCUMULATOR` cohorts vs `SNIPER` + `HIGH_FREQUENCY_TRADER`.

### Panel 2: Real-Time Bonding Curve Monitor (Per-Token Deep Dive)
- **Curve Fill Percentage**:
  $$\text{Curve Fill \%} = \min\left(100.0, \frac{\text{Cumulative Base Asset Collected}}{\text{Graduation Threshold Base}} \times 100\right)$$
- **Virtual AMM Depth Matrix**:
  Displays $x$ (virtual base reserve) and $y$ (virtual token reserve). Calculates expected slippage for orders of $0.5 \text{ BNB}$, $2 \text{ BNB}$, and $10 \text{ BNB}$.
- **Graduation Velocity Predictor**:
  Forecasts time remaining until migration based on rolling 15-minute base asset inflow rate:
  $$\text{Est. Minutes to Graduate} = \frac{\text{Base Remaining}}{\text{Inflow Rate per Minute}}$$

### Panel 3: Wallet Forensic & Risk Sentinel (Adversarial Detection)
- **Block 0–2 Sniper Penetration Index**:
  Percentage of initial token supply acquired within the first 2 blocks of curve creation.
  *Alert Trigger*: $>25\%$ triggers an orange risk tag on the frontend; $>40\%$ triggers an emergency warning.
- **Creator Dev Wallet Activity**:
  Tracks creator wallet balance and sells.
  *Alert Trigger*: If creator sells $>10\%$ of initial allocation before $80\%$ curve fill, flag as **"High Dump Risk"**.
- **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) for Token Concentration**:
  $$\text{HHI} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i^2$$
  where $s_i$ is the percentage share of circulating supply held by wallet $i$.
  - $\text{HHI} < 1,500$: Healthy Decentralized Distribution.
  - $1,500 \le \text{HHI} \le 2,500$: Moderate Concentration.
  - $\text{HHI} > 2,500$: High Cabal / Whale Risk.

### Panel 4: Token Velocity & Market Quality
- **Token Velocity Index ($V$)**:
  $$V_{24h} = \frac{\text{24h Trading Volume (Base)}}{\text{Circulating Market Cap (Base)}}$$
  - $V > 2.0$: **Hyper-Velocity** (High speculation, high bot churn, susceptible to rapid cooling).
  - $0.5 \le V \le 2.0$: **Healthy Circulation** (Optimal liquidity balance).
  - $V < 0.1$: **Stagnant Liquidity** (High risk of graduation failure).
- **Holder Retention Cohort Waterfall**:
  Tracks day 1, day 3, and day 7 retention of unique buyer addresses to measure true community stickiness.

## 4. Alerting & Webhook Rules

| Event Type | Condition | Notification Channel | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sniper Hijack** | Block 0-2 snipers acquire $>35\%$ total supply | Telegram Core Group / Discord Bot | High |
| **Dev Dumping** | Creator wallet sells $>5\%$ supply pre-graduation | Telegram Dev Channel | Critical |
| **Imminent Graduation** | Curve Fill reaches $90\%$ | Public Discord Announcement Bot | Info |
| **Whale Inflow** | Single transaction $>15\%$ of remaining graduation target | Core Trading Sentinel | Medium |
| **Protocol Revenue Spike** | Hourly fees $>3\times$ 7-day trailing average | Team Financial Dashboard | Info |
